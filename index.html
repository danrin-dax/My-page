I study Data Science and Analytics at Florida Atlantic University, with minors in Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity and a concentration in engineering. Right now I’m building two things: a clip-on reader that turns a finger on a printed page into a note on your phone, and a study app for Florida’s civic literacy exam.

Drag a box over any words on the page and they’ll land on the phone as a note. That’s what the reader does with a real book.

Next phase: the clip-on version. I’m building the reader into a small device that clips onto the top of a book like a reading light, so you can keep reading on paper without retyping anything. It captures text, tables, diagrams and photos, reads English and Spanish, and keeps your notes organized by book.

    A study app for the Florida Civic Literacy Exam, the civics test required at Florida’s public colleges and universities. It covers everything the exam can ask in short quizzes instead of long readings, and it stays current when the official requirements change.

    It’s built around the way I studied for and passed the exam myself, and you’ll be able to prepare all the way to a pass without paying.
